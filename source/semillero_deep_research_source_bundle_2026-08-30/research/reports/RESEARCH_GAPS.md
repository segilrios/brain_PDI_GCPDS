# Research Gaps and Opportunity Map

## Surface water
- Geographic/seasonal generalization remains more interesting than a single-region high accuracy score.
- Compare conventional segmentation baselines against EO foundation-model fine-tuning.
- Investigate optical + SAR robustness under clouds/flood conditions.
- Quantify temporal consistency and domain shift.

## Groundwater potential
- Validation quality is a primary gap: field aquifer-productivity evidence should be central.
- Explicit uncertainty and calibration are underdeveloped.
- Transferability across geology/climate regions remains difficult.
- XAI should be geologically meaningful, not merely a post-hoc visualization.
- Physics-informed inverse modeling offers a route from proxy mapping toward parameter inference.

## Geothermal
- Surface EO evidence needs stronger integration with geophysics/geochemistry and reservoir evidence.
- Standardized validation and uncertainty frameworks remain weak.
- Hybrid expert + statistical + ensemble ML approaches are promising.
- XAI can help test whether model-important variables are geologically plausible.
- Research opportunity: connect prospectivity to resource/reservoir and techno-economic uncertainty.

## Knowledge brain
- Prevent citation graph centrality from being mistaken for scientific importance.
- Treat Claim and Evidence as first-class nodes/records.
- Preserve contradictory claims, negative results and retractions.
- Keep all derived views/indexes rebuildable from Git-tracked canonical records.
