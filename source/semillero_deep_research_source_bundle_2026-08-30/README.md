## Copyright / full-text policy
This archive does **not** redistribute publisher-controlled paper PDFs. It stores metadata, DOI/official URLs, synthesis, provenance-oriented notes and open-source links. During APPLY, add legally available/local researcher-supplied PDFs to a Git-ignored local library and register exact evidence locators.

## Scientific caution
The archive intentionally distinguishes direct surface-water observation from groundwater-potential inference and geothermal prospectivity. A surface proxy is not automatically subsurface confirmation.
