# Source and Licensing Note

- Publisher-controlled full-text articles are represented by bibliographic metadata and official/DOI links, not redistributed PDFs.
- Open-source repositories are linked to their official GitHub pages; this bundle does not vendor entire repositories.
- Open datasets are linked to their official provider/catalog pages; large data bytes are not embedded.
- The bundle's generated summaries, catalogs, manifests and candidate graph files are intended as project research metadata.
- Before using a source, dataset, model or repository in a publication or implementation, re-check its current license, version, citation instructions and access conditions.
